## Challenge Craft 1.1.4 — Minecraft 26.3

*Fixes since 1.1.3.*

### Fixes

- **Infinite Chest:** what you take out or put in by clicking is now always saved — before, a reload could bring taken items back or lose items you put in.
- **Infinite Chest:** only the chest you have open and in reach reacts to your clicks, and its screen closes when you walk away or the chest is broken.
- **Infinite Chest:** a left click takes one stack of that item (one sword, 16 ender pearls), no longer 64.
- **Crafter:** no longer crafts the Infinite Chest or the casino devices — they keep their level and purchase requirements, so craft them by hand.
- **Dice:** you keep your rolled blocks when someone joins a LAN world or the challenge settings change.
- **The House Always Wins:** the roulette table and the cashier check every request properly.
- The thrown die shows its name.
