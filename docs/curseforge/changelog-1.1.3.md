## Challenge Craft 1.1.3 — Minecraft 26.3

*Everything new since 1.1.2.*

### New challenge

- 🕳️ **Void Trail** (Level 11) — every block you leave takes its whole column with it, from the build limit down to the void: bedrock, water and lava included. Only End portal frames and the End portal survive, so the run stays winnable. There is no way back — keep moving onto fresh ground.

### Fixes

- **Force Item Battle:** the team screen shows its buttons again (teams, Play Solo, Start Battle, Use Joker) and the lobby lists everyone online, not only players who already joined a team.
- **Force Item Battle:** the chain is back in the item pool (it is the iron chain in 26.3).
- **Tutorial:** the highlight ring fits its box at every GUI scale.

### Bob, the Lockout rival *(Early Development Stage)*

- Takes the quick tiles first and only leaps where falling short is harmless.
- Survives much better on Hard: cover in time, a shield against arrows, nights dug in when they are dangerous, creepers hunted for their powder.
- Ships with models trained in Bob-against-Bob matches.
